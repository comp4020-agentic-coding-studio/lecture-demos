Who you know

Sixteen were out of reach: eleven that only Blue had, four that only evolve part-way through a trade, and Mew, which normal play never offered at all. Gotta catch 'em all was the slogan for a cartridge that shipped eleven short and four impossible. The gap wasn't a bug in the product — it was the product.
